Applied Scientist · AWS Generative AI Innovation Center

Mengdie (Flora) Wang

I build generative-AI systems — agentic workflows and beyond — and study whether you can trust what they produce.

I build GenAI systems for enterprise customers and take them from discovery into production. Alongside the build, I study whether these systems can be trusted — quantifying their uncertainty and reasoning consistency with calibration and other statistical methods, so a system knows when an answer can be acted on and when it should be escalated. And I turn what I learn in the field into research papers, AWS blogs, workshops, and reusable implementation patterns.

  1. 2026
    EMNLP 2026 · Main Track

    From Debate to Decision: Conformal Social Choice for Safe Multi-Agent Deliberation

    M. F. Wang, H. Xie, G. Wang, A. Gao, G. Yang, Z. Li, Q. W. Qiu, F. Han, H. Qiu, Y. Huang, B. Zhu, J. O. Woo

    A post-hoc safety layer for multi-agent LLM systems that converts agent debate into calibrated act-or-escalate decisions — intercepting 81.9% of wrong-consensus cases while preserving high autonomous-action accuracy.

    ICLR 2026 · Workshop 🏆 Best Paper Award

    Quantifying Consistency in LLM Logical Reasoning via Structural Uncertainty

    B. Chaudhury, M. F. Wang, H. H. Park, R. Ghosh, S. Hong, J. O. Woo

    Consistency-aware evaluation for black-box LLM reasoning using ranking distributions, Bradley–Terry modeling, and PageRank-style aggregation to detect unreliable reasoning instances.

    Findings of EACL 2026

    Reflect, Rewrite, Repeat: How Simple Arithmetic Enables Advanced Reasoning in Small Language Models

    M. F. Wang, H. Xie, M. Y. Kim, B. Chaudhury, M. Ashok, S. Gunturu, S. Hong, J. O. Woo

    Built the R3 pipeline (SFT + DPO) improving small-language-model reasoning — AIME’25@10 from 20% to 30% with 700 training samples — producing high-fidelity reasoning traces for annotation-light fine-tuning.

    🎤 Basis for briefing the LG U+ CTO Tech Review on data-efficient model distillation and deployment.
