Reliable agentic AI.
From research methods to working systems.

I am an applied scientist specializing in reliable agentic AI. I study how to evaluate reasoning, quantify uncertainty, and decide when an AI system should act, ask, or defer.

I pair this research with hands-on work on agent systems, evaluation pipelines, and model customization—taking ideas into enterprise applications.

EMNLP 2026 · Main Track

From Debate to Decision

When agents agree, is their answer safe to act on?

A post-hoc safety layer turns multi-agent deliberation into calibrated act-or-escalate decisions. The evaluation intercepted 81.9% of wrong-consensus cases while preserving high autonomous-action accuracy.

Method: Conformal social choice for multi-agent reliability.

ICLR 2026 · WorkshopBest Paper Award

Quantifying Consistency in LLM Logical Reasoning

Can inconsistent reasoning reveal unreliable answers?

Consistency-aware evaluation for black-box LLM reasoning uses ranking distributions, Bradley–Terry modeling, and PageRank-style aggregation to identify unreliable reasoning instances.

Method: Structural uncertainty and reasoning-consistency evaluation.

Findings of EACL 2026

Reflect, Rewrite, Repeat

How far can a small model go with limited reasoning data?

Built the R3 pipeline using supervised fine-tuning and direct preference optimization. With 700 training samples, the pipeline improved AIME’25@10 from 20% to 30%, producing reasoning traces for annotation-light fine-tuning.

Implementation: Data generation, fine-tuning, and reasoning evaluation in the R3 pipeline.

Problem. Support scientific question answering with retrieval and agent orchestration.

Outcome. The engagement secured a production phase.

Source-cited analytics through a semantic layer

Problem. Make a sprawling data warehouse usable for traceable, natural-language analytics.

My contribution. Distilled the warehouse into a governed semantic layer with a FastAPI and Claude retrieval service for source-cited answers.

Focus. Connecting data meaning, retrieval, and auditable responses.
